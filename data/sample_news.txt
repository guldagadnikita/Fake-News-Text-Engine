The Pune Municipal Corporation announced that several major roads across the city will undergo comprehensive maintenance work this week. According to official statements released by the municipal commissioner, the maintenance will be carried out in three phases to minimize traffic disruption during peak hours.

The spokesperson confirmed that the project involves resurfacing of damaged stretches, repair of potholes, and improvement of drainage systems along key arterial roads. Published data shows that the city has allocated a budget of approximately 45 crore rupees for this infrastructure improvement initiative.

Researchers at the Indian Institute of Technology Pune have provided technical consultation for the project, recommending sustainable materials for road construction. Citizens have been advised to use alternative routes during peak hours and check the official PMC website for daily updates on road closures and diversions.

The investigation into previous road maintenance contracts has been completed, and the documented findings suggest improved quality control measures are now in place. A press release from the municipal office confirmed that all contractors have been verified and selected through a transparent bidding process.
